# Phase 6: attachments and audio

The shared composer is used by Messages and Conversations. It supports paste, files, native camera when available, photo input fallback, Drive selection, voice preview and recording cancellation. Images have fitted thumbnails and a full-screen viewer. Audio keeps its original, produces browser-safe playback, exposes duration and speed, and indexes completed transcripts.

Eight unique media browser cases passed. Both profiles are covered: iPhone 390 by 844, device scale factor 3 and touch, and desktop 1440 by 900. Coverage includes inbound voice playback at 1.5x with advancing audio time, transcript search, photo attachment, pasted images, files and Drive links, a synthetic recorded voice send, and recording cancellation and restart. The phone cancellation case uses actual browser touch events for hold, left-slide cancellation and release to preview. The desktop case uses click controls. No cancelled recording enters the outbox.

The lead viewed all 12 final screenshots in `evidence/messages/p6`. The stems `voice-player`, `transcript-search`, `photo-attached`, `drive-picker`, `file-and-link` and `voice-preview` each have an `-iphone.png` and `-desktop.png` capture. They show complete playback controls and transcript, visible shell and conversation headers, and the composer within the viewport.
